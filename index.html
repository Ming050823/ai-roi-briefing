3. 報告核心發現

3. Core findings of the note

3.1 企業系統性高估自己的落地速度

3.1 Firms systematically overestimate their own speed

一年前同一批受訪者被問「多久能規模化」時：

A year earlier, the same respondent pool was asked how long it would take to reach scaled deployment:

  • 10% 說 3 個月內；19% 說 6 個月內；13% 說 9 個月內；43% 說 12 個月內
  • 10% said within 3 months; 19% within 6; 13% within 9; 43% within 12 months

合計 84% 認為自己一年內能做到。一年後真正跨過門檻的只有約 5%。報告寫明：這種樂觀偏差每一輪調查都出現，而且預期與現實的落差還在擴大。圖中紅藍兩條線越拉越開，就是這件事。

In total, 84% expected to get there within a year. About 5% actually crossed the threshold. The note says this optimism bias appears in every wave, and the gap is still widening. That is what the diverging lines on Figure 1 are showing.

3.3 對市場的含義

3.3 What it means for markets

市場對 AI 的定價，很多建立在「企業會很快大規模採用」上。這份調查認為這個假設本身就被高估了，而且企業自己也在高估。技術可用 ≠ 企業規模化落地。中間還隔著 ROI 驗證、系統集成、人才、合規。模型變強，這些障礙不會自動消失。對投資人來說，要分開看「AI 能力進步速度」和「AI 商業落地速度」。

A lot of AI pricing assumes enterprises will scale quickly. This survey says that assumption is itself inflated — and that firms inflate it about themselves. “The model works” is not “the firm has scaled.” ROI proof, integration, talent and compliance sit in between. Stronger models do not dissolve those barriers. Investors need to separate the speed of capability gains from the speed of commercial deployment.

2026 年 2 月 Kapteyn 也用同一套數據反駁「美國生產率上升已經能用 AI 普及解釋」：當時仍有八成以上企業未把 AI 真正嵌進生產流程，資本支出與招聘行為也還沒出現對應轉向。

In February 2026 Kapteyn used the same series to reject the claim that a US productivity uptick already proved widespread AI deployment: more than 80% of firms had still not embedded AI in production processes, and capex and hiring had not shifted in a matching way.

4. 六大落地障礙細節

4. The six barriers in detail

這六項不是六個獨立問題，而是同一條「試點 → 規模化」路徑上的連續卡點。

These are not six separate problems. They are consecutive choke points on the same pilot-to-scale path.

4.1 ROI 不清晰（53%）——不是「沒效益」，是「算不出、對不上、批不下去」

4.1 Unclear ROI (53%) — not “no benefit,” but “cannot price it, cannot map it, cannot fund it”

這是整份調查最穩定的第一名。2025 年 3 月約 50%，10 月升到 59%，2026 年 3 月回落到 53%，始終壓過技術問題。

This is the most stable number-one item. It was about 50% in March 2025, 59% in October 2025, and 53% in March 2026 — always above purely technical issues.

現場常見三種算帳失敗：

Three kinds of accounting failure show up in practice:

  • 試點指標和財報指標對不上。回覆速度、補全接受率、會議摘要使用量很好看，但 CFO 要的是單位成本、週轉、客訴、毛利。IBM 後來量到企業 AI 平均 ROI 約 5.9%，低於多數公司約 10% 的資金成本，正是「有活動、沒利潤」。
  • Pilot metrics do not map to financial statements. Reply speed, autocomplete accept rates and meeting-summary usage look strong; CFOs want unit cost, cycle time, complaints and margin. IBM later put average enterprise AI ROI near 5.9%, below a typical ~10% cost of capital — activity without profit.
  • 成本結構被低估。試點只算 API／token；規模化還要算資料清洗、權限、監控、人審、事故、合規、舊系統改造。Bain 2026 年發現不少公司用「上一輪自動化省下來的錢」去養下一輪 AI，但上一輪本來就低於預期。
  • The cost stack is under-counted. Pilots price APIs and tokens. Scale also prices cleaning, permissions, monitoring, human review, incidents, compliance and legacy rewiring. Bain’s 2026 work found many firms funding the next AI wave from prior automation savings that had already missed target.
  • 時間窗錯了。能算出帳的專案通常要 6–12 個月才看得到穩定效益，董事會卻用 1–2 個季度的試點數字決定加碼。紅線（一年前 84% 自以為一年內能規模化）本質上也是 ROI 樂觀：把「模型能做」當成「公司能賺錢」。
  • The time window is wrong. Projects that can be priced usually need 6–12 months before the benefit stabilises; boards often decide from one or two pilot quarters. The maroon line — 84% expecting scale inside a year — is ROI optimism in another form: treating “the model can do it” as “the firm will make money.”
本質：衡量體系還沒從「模型演示」換成「流程單位經濟」。沒有這套帳，後續五項再怎麼改善，預算仍會在規模化門口停住。
The real issue: measurement has not moved from model demos to workflow unit economics. Without that ledger, fixing the other five barriers still leaves budget stuck at the scale gate.

4.2 合規與監管（48%）——審批節奏比模型迭代慢一個數量級

4.2 Compliance and regulation (48%) — approval cycles lag model cycles by an order of magnitude

IT 與資料工程決策者碰到的不是「有沒有 AI 法案」這種標題，而是日常過不了關：模型能不能碰客戶個資與交易資料；輸出要不要可解釋、要不要留審計軌跡；跨境資料能不能進哪一家雲、哪一個模型；agent 一旦能自行改訂單、發郵件、調權限，責任算誰的。

IT and data leaders are not mainly debating the existence of an AI Act. They are failing day-to-day gates: whether a model may touch customer or transaction data; whether outputs must be explainable and auditable; which cloud and which model may receive cross-border data; and who is accountable when an agent can change an order, send mail, or alter permissions.

一個 demo 兩週能做完；同一套東西進生產，法務、資安、模型風險、採購、內審可能要排 4–6 個月。買方研究機構 2026 年對大型資產管理公司的調查也顯示：把研究資料接進內部 AI，最大障礙是授權與合規資格（約 69%、54%），不是模型本身。

A demo can ship in two weeks. The same system in production can spend 4–6 months in legal, security, model risk, procurement and audit. A 2026 buy-side study of large asset managers found the top barriers to feeding research into internal AI were licensing and compliance entitlements (~69% and ~54%), not the model.

還有結構問題：企業 AI 用得越廣，合規部門自己用 AI 的成熟度越跟不上。有研究寫過，企業已「廣泛或進階採用 AI」的約 67%，合規職能自己達到同等水準的只有 22%。審核者比被審核者更慢，閘門就會越拉越緊。

There is also a structural lag: the more widely the enterprise uses AI, the further compliance itself falls behind. One study found about 67% of organisations at broad or advanced enterprise AI adoption, versus 22% of ethics-and-compliance functions at the same level. When reviewers are slower than the reviewed, gates tighten.

合規是「能不能上線、誰簽字」；隱私是「資料能不能進模型」。一個卡流程，一個卡原料。

Compliance is “may this go live, and who signs.” Privacy is “may this data enter the model.” One blocks process; the other blocks feedstock.

4.3 集成複雜度（45%，前兩輪 37–38%）——唯一明顯惡化的一項

4.3 Integration complexity (45%, up from 37–38%) — the only item clearly getting worse

前兩輪很多人還在做獨立聊天視窗、獨立 Copilot；這一輪開始要把 AI 接進 ERP、CRM、核心交易、工單、權限系統，複雜度才真正露面。「集成」通常裂成四層，任何一層沒接上，試點都過不了生產：

Earlier waves were still full of standalone chat windows and copilots. This wave is about wiring AI into ERP, CRM, core transactions, tickets and permission systems. That is when complexity appears. Integration usually splits into four layers; miss any one and the pilot dies in production:

  1. 系統層：舊核心沒有穩定 API，試點用整理過的沙盒，生產面對不一致的 schema 與延遲。
  2. Systems: legacy cores lack stable APIs; pilots run on cleaned sandboxes; production faces inconsistent schemas and latency.
  3. 流程層：AI 輸出之後，人怎麼接、例外怎麼升、錯了怎麼回滾。把 agent 接到工作流，不是把模型掛在舊流程旁邊，而是整段流程要重畫。
  4. Process: after the model writes, who picks up, how exceptions escalate, how errors roll back. Wiring an agent into a workflow is not hanging a model beside the old process; the process itself has to be redrawn.
  5. 權限層：人有角色權限；模型預設「什麼都看得到」。一上生產就要做欄位級、文件級、客戶級隔離。
  6. Permissions: people have roles; models default to seeing everything. Production needs field-, document- and customer-level isolation.
  7. 組織層：IT 能上線工具，業務不改考核、不改 SOP，使用率會停在「偶爾試試看」。
  8. Organisation: IT can ship a tool; if the business does not change incentives and SOPs, usage stalls at “we tried it once.”

Alteryx 2026 年調查把「無法嵌入既有系統與工作流」列為試點轉生產的第一障礙（52%）。占比會升，是因為「已經開始認真推」的公司變多了。沒推的人覺得集成不難；推到一半的人才會把它勾成障礙。Bain 也觀察到：達標的公司反而更常把「資料取得與整合」列為最大牆——不是他們比較差，是他們撞牆撞得更前面。

Alteryx’s 2026 survey put “cannot embed into existing systems and workflows” as the top barrier from pilot to production (52%). The share rises because more firms are now pushing in earnest. Firms that have not pushed still think integration is easy; firms halfway through tick the box. Bain saw the same pattern: firms that hit targets cite data access and integration more often — not because they are weaker, but because they hit the wall earlier.

這也解釋圖上那條幾乎直線的藍線：每半年只有約 3% 的公司跨過門檻，卡的就是這層工程與組織改造，不是下一個模型版本。

That is also why the blue line is almost straight: only about 3% of firms cross the threshold each half-year. The bind is engineering and organisational rewiring, not the next model version.

4.4 合格人才短缺（42%）——缺的不是「會寫 prompt 的人」

4.4 Talent shortage (42%) — not a shortage of people who can write prompts

缺的通常是三種很難同時存在的人：能把業務問題拆成可上線系統的人（產品＋工程＋領域）；能把髒資料、權限、評估、監控做成平台的人（MLOps／資料平台）；能在業務側改流程、扛結果的人（領域專家，不是 IT）。

What is scarce is three profiles that rarely exist in one person: someone who can turn a business problem into a production system (product + engineering + domain); someone who can turn dirty data, permissions, evaluation and monitoring into a platform (MLOps / data platform); and someone on the business side who will redesign the process and own the outcome (a domain owner, not IT).

2025 年 10 月那波瑞銀調查裡，約 60% 企業選擇自建或混搭。自建直接把人才缺口放大：買現成至少把一部分工程外包出去；自建等於自己養整合、治理、評估團隊。許多公司的應對卻是「開課提高 AI 素養」，而不是改職位設計。結果是人人都會用 Copilot，但沒有人負責把某個端到端流程從「人做」改成「人＋系統做」。

In the October 2025 UBS wave, about 60% of firms built in-house or used a hybrid model. Building in-house widens the talent gap: buying off the shelf outsources some engineering; building means you staff integration, governance and evaluation yourself. Many firms respond with fluency courses rather than job redesign. Everyone can use a copilot; nobody owns turning an end-to-end process from “done by people” into “done by people plus a system.”

人才短缺和 ROI 互鎖：算不清帳就不敢開正式編制；沒有正式編制就繼續靠專案組打游擊；游擊隊做得出 demo，做不出可維運的生產系統。瑞銀 2026 年 2 月筆記也用「招聘行為沒有明顯轉向」反證：如果真的在規模化，勞動力結構應該先動。

Talent and ROI lock together: if the ledger is unclear, headcount is not opened; without headcount, work stays in guerrilla project teams; guerrilla teams can demo, they cannot run production. Kapteyn’s February 2026 note used “no clear shift in hiring” as counter-evidence: if scale were real, labour mix would move first.

4.5 資料可用性與品質（42%）——生產和試點用的不是同一份資料

4.5 Data availability and quality (42%) — production and the pilot are not using the same data

試點資料範圍小、有人清過、允許錯、不接即時系統。生產資料分散在十幾個系統、定義不一致、時效不同、缺值有業務含義、權限割裂。所以「資料問題」通常不是「沒有大數據」，而是四件事同時成立：找不到、對不上、不能用、進不去。

Pilot data is narrow, cleaned, allowed to be wrong, and disconnected from live systems. Production data sits in a dozen systems, with inconsistent definitions, mismatched freshness, missingness that means something, and fractured permissions. So “the data problem” is rarely “we lack big data.” It is four things at once: cannot find it, cannot join it, cannot trust it for action, cannot legally send it.

McKinsey 對 agentic AI 的判斷很硬：約八成企業把「資料限制」列為規模化最大障礙，不是模型。Teradata 2026 年更具體：77% 的主管說，企業裡只有不到 20% 的資料和知識，可靠到能讓 agent 據此行動。這就是為什麼 Copilot 類「輔助寫字」容易鋪開，訂單／理賠／調度類「自動做事」極難鋪開。

McKinsey has been blunt on agents: about eight in ten firms name data limits, not models, as the binding constraint on scale. Teradata in 2026 was more specific: 77% of leaders said 20% or less of enterprise data and knowledge is reliable enough for an agent to act on. That is why writing copilots spread and why order / claims / scheduling agents do not.

4.6 資料隱私（37%）——原料被鎖在合規櫃裡

4.6 Data privacy (37%) — the feedstock is locked in the compliance cabinet

現場典型情況：客戶合約、員工檔、原始交易不能進公有雲大模型；可以進，但必須先去識別，去識別後效果掉一截，ROI 更難看；供應商條款禁止把內容用於訓練，卻說不清推理快取算不算訓練；模型輸出可能把 A 客戶的內容帶進 B 客戶的回答。

Typical cases: contracts, employee files and raw transactions cannot enter a public frontier model; they can enter only after de-identification, which weakens performance and makes ROI worse; vendor terms forbid training use but leave inference caches ambiguous; outputs can leak Client A into Client B’s answer.

這會把企業推進兩條貴且慢的路：自建／私有化部署，或在資料倉上只做「摘要級」應用。兩條路都會拉高集成成本和人才需求，並讓「規模化」定義變窄——很多公司最後只敢上內部 IT 客服、寫文件、寫程式，不敢上核心客戶流程。瑞銀前一波調查裡，內部 IT help desk 的 AI 需求（約 75%）明顯高於外部客服（約 52%），和隱私邊界高度一致。

That pushes firms onto two expensive, slow paths: private / self-hosted deployment, or summary-only use on top of the warehouse. Both raise integration cost and talent demand, and they narrow what “scale” means — many firms end up on internal IT help desks, drafting and coding, and stay off core customer flows. In an earlier UBS wave, demand for AI on the internal IT help desk (~75%) ran well ahead of external customer support (~52%), which tracks the privacy boundary.

6. 如何量測 AI 的 ROI

6. How to measure AI ROI

瑞銀把「ROI 不清晰」排第一，不是因為公式難寫，而是大多數公司量錯了單位。McKinsey 2026 年調查裡，八成人說 AI 讓自己更有效率，但只有 37% 能把影響接到 EBIT，能把 EBIT 的 5% 以上歸因於 AI 的只有約 6%。個人有感、公司沒帳，就是量測失敗。

UBS ranks “unclear ROI” first not because the formula is hard, but because most firms measure the wrong unit. In McKinsey’s 2026 survey, eight in ten people said AI made them more productive, yet only 37% could attach any EBIT effect, and only about 6% could attribute 5% or more of EBIT to AI. Individual sensation without a corporate ledger is a measurement failure.

核心原則：不要量工具，要量「一筆被接受的完成工作」花了多少錢、換回多少錢。
Core rule: do not measure the tool. Measure what one accepted completed job costs, and what it returns.

6.1 先改單位，再算百分比

6.1 Change the unit before you compute a percentage

錯誤單位：prompt 次數、座位數、token 費、滿意度問卷、「我覺得省了 30 分鐘」。正確單位：一張發票、一張工單、一份合約、一次理賠、一次程式變更、一通已解決的客服。

Wrong units: prompt counts, seats, token bills, satisfaction surveys, “I feel I saved 30 minutes.” Right units: one invoice, one ticket, one contract, one claim, one code change, one resolved customer case.

ROI = (V − C) / C

難的是 V 和 C 都必須落在同一條工作流上。更好用的日常指標不是年度 ROI，而是：

The hard part is that both V and C must sit on the same workflow. A more useful daily metric than annual ROI is:

cost per accepted outcome = fully loaded workflow cost / number of accepted completions

「被接受」三個字最重要：生成了但不被採用、要重做、要人改半小時，都不能算完成。Cost per token 回答「系統處理了什麼」；cost per accepted outcome 回答「系統做成了什麼」。

“Accepted” is the word that matters. Generated-but-unused, reworked, or edited for half an hour does not count as done. Cost per token answers what the system processed. Cost per accepted outcome answers what the system finished.

6.4 上線前一定要有基線

6.4 You must have a baseline before go-live

每條要規模化的工作流，上線前至少鎖四個基線，用 4–8 週營運數據，不要用主管估計：週期時間；一次通過率／錯誤率／重工率；單件全負載成本；單位時間吞吐。沒有基線，就沒有反事實。

For every workflow you intend to scale, lock four baselines from 4–8 weeks of operational data, not manager estimates: cycle time; first-pass / error / rework rate; fully loaded cost per unit; throughput per unit time. No baseline means no counterfactual.

  • 能切流量：A/B 或分批上線
  • If you can split traffic: A/B or staged rollout
  • 不能切：上線前後＋控制同期其他變化（旺季、新人、流程改版）
  • If you cannot: before/after plus controls for concurrent changes (season, new hires, process rewrites)
  • 高風險決策：人機對照，記錄覆寫原因
  • High-stakes decisions: human–machine paired review, with override reasons logged

人做 2 小時、核對 6 分鐘，比值約 5%。Agent 只要超過約 5% 的次數做對，淨效益就可以為正——前提是核對真的只要 6 分鐘，而且失敗代價已被算進 V_adjusted。任務定義不清、核對變成重做，這個不等式立刻翻轉。

If a person needs two hours to do the work and six minutes to verify it, the ratio is about 5%. The agent only needs to succeed a bit more than five times in a hundred to be net positive — provided verification really is six minutes and failure costs are already inside V_adjusted. If the task is undefined and verification becomes rework, the inequality flips immediately.

7. 90 天起步方法與例子

7. A 90-day start and a worked example

一條工作流跑通，比開二十個試點有用。

One finished workflow is worth more than twenty pilots.

  1. 第 0 週｜選題。只選「件數夠、基線在、單件有價、例外可定義」的流程。例如：每月 >1,000 件的 L1 工單、應付憑證、合約初審、程式碼 review。
  2. Week 0 · Pick the job. Choose a flow with enough volume, an existing baseline, a priced unit, and defined exceptions. Examples: L1 tickets above 1,000 a month, AP invoices, first-pass contract review, code review.
  3. 第 1–2 週｜鎖基線與成功定義。寫成一句話：「把 X 的單件全負載成本從 a 降到 b，一次通過率從 p1 升到 p2，回收期不超過 n 個月。」業務負責人簽字。
  4. Weeks 1–2 · Lock baseline and success. One sentence: “Move fully loaded unit cost of X from a to b, first-pass yield from p1 to p2, payback inside n months.” The business owner signs it.
  5. 第 3–4 週｜把成本打到這條流上。推論、人審、集成工時、平台分攤都掛同一張成本中心。
  6. Weeks 3–4 · Put cost on that flow. Inference, human review, integration hours and platform allocation all sit on one cost centre.
  7. 第 5–12 週｜只看被接受的產出。每週看完成件數、單件成本、覆寫原因前五名、失敗重跑成本。覆寫原因是產品待辦，不是註腳。
  8. Weeks 5–12 · Watch accepted output only. Weekly: completions, unit cost, top five override reasons, rerun cost. Override reasons are product backlog, not footnotes.
  9. 第 13 週｜三種決策。擴：單件成本連續下降且採用穩定。停：層 B 無改善。改範圍：模型沒問題，卡在資料、權限或 SOP——不應被記成「AI 沒 ROI」。
  10. Week 13 · Three decisions. Scale: unit cost is falling and adoption is stable. Stop: Layer B is flat. Rescope: the model is fine and the bind is data, permissions or SOP — that is not “AI has no ROI.”

客服偏轉的數字例子

Worked example: support deflection

基線：人工關單一件 $9.50，月 20,000 件。上線後：agent 月費與推論 $32,000，偏轉 18,000 件，剩下 2,000 件仍走人工；被偏轉件中 12% 因錯誤回流，回流按人工件計。

Baseline: $9.50 to close a ticket by hand, 20,000 tickets a month. After go-live: agent platform and inference cost $32,000 a month, 18,000 tickets deflected, 2,000 still handled by people; 12% of deflected tickets bounce back and are costed as human tickets.

effective deflections = 18,000 × (1 − 0.12) = 15,840 V = 15,840 × 9.50 = $150,480 C = 32,000 + 2,000 × 9.50 = $51,000 monthly net = $99,480 ROI = 99,480 / 51,000 ≈ 195%

若把回流當成「已成功」、或不算人審，數字會再漂亮一截，也會在第二個季度被拆穿。把回流、覆寫、重跑留在公式裡，帳才能跟瑞銀說的「規模化」對上：只有單件經濟穩定優於基線的流程，才配從 19% 那條藍線往上走。

Treat bounce-backs as successes, or omit human review, and the number looks better — until the second quarter. Keep bounce-backs, overrides and reruns in the formula and the ledger matches what UBS means by “scale”: only workflows whose unit economics beat the baseline on a sustained basis deserve to climb the 19% blue line.

最小可行量測，三個數字就夠起步：（1）這條工作流現在單件花多少錢；（2）上 AI 後，被接受的單件花多少錢；（3）差額有沒有進費用或產能，而不是停在「大家覺得比較快」。這三個數字能按月更新，ROI 就不清晰不了；更新不了，問題通常不在公式，而在還沒選到一條真正能量的工作流。
A minimum viable measurement is three numbers: (1) what a unit costs on this workflow today; (2) what an accepted unit costs after AI; (3) whether the gap hits expenses or capacity, rather than stopping at “people feel faster.” If those three numbers can be refreshed monthly, ROI is no longer unclear. If they cannot, the problem is usually not the formula. It is that you have not yet chosen a workflow that can be measured.

8. 深入：ROI 量測方法論

8. Deep dive: ROI measurement methods

上一章講的是「單位要改成被接受的完成件」。這一章往下走一層：量測是一套歸因系統，公式只是最後一行。瑞銀把 ROI 列第一；Futurum 2026 也寫過：財務衝擊已取代生產力成為第一成功指標，但真正能量出財務衝擊的企業大約只有 5–8%。缺口不在模型，在量測基礎設施。

The previous chapter changed the unit to an accepted completed job. This chapter goes one layer down: measurement is an attribution system; the formula is only the last line. UBS ranks unclear ROI first. Futurum’s 2026 survey likewise found that direct financial impact has replaced productivity as the top success metric — yet only about 5–8% of firms can actually measure financial impact. The gap is infrastructure, not models.

  • L2 低於門檻，L3–L5 不准報正 ROI。Copilot 類採用率長期低於約 60%，總帳通常打不平。
  • If L2 is below the gate, L3–L5 may not report positive ROI. Copilot-class tools that sit below ~60% adoption rarely break even in aggregate.
  • 只有 L4 連續為正，才允許談 L5 的「規模化」。 這正好對上瑞銀藍線：跨多個業務單位、穩定在生產。
  • Only a run of positive L4 allows talk of L5 “scale.” That matches the UBS blue line: production at scale across multiple units.

Agent 還要多看 Forrester 的「行動落差」：洞察到動作之間隔了幾個人、幾個系統。落差越大，L1 再好也走不到 L4——價值死在最後一哩的人工搬運。

Agents also need Forrester’s “action gap”: how many people and systems sit between an insight and an action. The larger the gap, the less a green L1 can reach L4 — value dies in the last mile of manual carrying.

8.3 歸因：怎麼證明「是 AI，不是別的」

8.3 Attribution: proving it was AI, not something else

沒有歸因，ROI 只是相關。企業能用的方法按嚴謹度排：

Without attribution, ROI is only correlation. Methods available to a firm, ranked by rigour:

1. 隨機或準實驗（金標準）

1. Randomised or quasi-experiments (gold standard)

能切流量就切。同一工作流、同一時期，AI 組 vs 對照組。銷售轉換、推薦、定價、客服話術都適用。對照組必須是「本來也可以上、只是沒上」的單位，不是「技術上根本用不上」的單位，否則是選擇偏差。實驗要夠長，蓋過學習曲線：前兩週常是負的，第 6–10 週才穩。

Split traffic when you can. Same workflow, same period, AI versus control. Conversion, recommendations, pricing and support scripts all fit. The control group must be units that could have adopted and did not — not units that were technically ineligible, or you have selection bias. Run long enough to cover the learning curve: the first two weeks are often negative; weeks 6–10 are when the series usually settles.

2. 分批上線／雙差（次佳）

2. Staged rollout / difference-in-differences (next best)

不能隨機時：先上 A 團隊、後上 B 團隊，看 A 相對 B 的增量，而不是看 A 相對自己的過去。

When you cannot randomise: ship to team A first and team B later. Measure A’s lift relative to B, not A relative to its own past.

Δ_DiD = (Y_A,after − Y_A,before) − (Y_B,after − Y_B,before)

同時發生的旺季、系統升級、考核改版，會在第二項被減掉。這是多數內部流程唯一做得到的「準因果」。

Seasonality, system upgrades and incentive changes that hit both groups drop out in the second term. For most internal processes this is the only quasi-causal design that is actually available.

3. 關掉測試（因果的壓力測試）

3. A turn-it-off test (a stress test for causation)

工程類特別有用：同一 repo、同一流程，停用 AI 7 天。若週期、重工率明顯回退，才能說前面的改善不是同時發生的人事或流程變化。這是少數能在董事會上防守的現場實驗。

Especially useful in engineering: same repo, same flow, AI off for seven days. If cycle time and rework regress, the earlier gain was not a concurrent staffing or process change. This is one of the few field tests that survives a board challenge.

4. 保守歸屬（無法實驗時的下限）

4. Conservative attribution (a lower bound when you cannot experiment)

產出是人機混成時，預設歸人，不歸 AI。只有能追溯到「這一筆是模型生成且未被實質改寫」的部分，才進 AI 的 V。有團隊在程式碼層做行級歸屬：不確定的行直接排除。數字會變小，但過得了審計。反過來說，現況是大量 AI 輔助產出在系統裡仍只記成「員工完成」，AI 的貢獻從未進入既有績效數字。這不是哲學問題，是帳簿設計問題。

When output is mixed human-and-model, default credit to the person, not the model. Only the slice that can be traced as “generated by the model and not materially rewritten” enters AI’s V. Some teams attribute at line level in code and drop uncertain lines. The number gets smaller; it also survives audit. In the opposite direction, a large share of AI-assisted output is still recorded only as “completed by an employee,” so the software never enters the performance numbers the firm already keeps. That is a ledger-design problem, not a philosophical one.

歸因協議要在上線前寫死：這條流的改善，多少算 AI、多少算同時上線的知識庫／排班系統。事後補協議，歸因一定死。
Write the attribution protocol before go-live: how much of this flow’s improvement is AI, and how much is the knowledge base or roster system that shipped in the same window. Retrofitting the protocol after launch is where attribution dies.

8.4 單位經濟：不要再從 token 起算

8.4 Unit economics: stop starting from the token

Token 是帳單，不是價值。銀行客服類 agent 的可變成本裡，token 常常只占 20–25%，人審與風控占 70–75%。用降 token 單價當「AI 變便宜了」，會看錯槓桿。

Tokens are the bill, not the value. In banking-style support agents, tokens often make up only 20–25% of variable run cost; human review and risk make up 70–75%. Treating a lower token price as “AI got cheaper” looks at the wrong lever.

c_unit = (c_inference + c_platform + c_orchestration + c_review + c_rerun + c_incident) / N_accepted unit net = v_unit − c_unit

v_unit 是這件工作在舊流程裡的全負載成本，或它帶來的可歸因毛利，兩者取你真的能防守的那個。

v_unit is either the fully loaded cost of the old process for that job, or the attributable gross margin the job creates — whichever number you can actually defend.

三個會讓 c_unit 看起來很美、實際為負的陷阱：

Three traps that make c_unit look beautiful and actually negative:

  1. 分母用「生成件」不是「接受件」。 生成 100、改寫 50，成功率是 50%，不是 100%。
  2. The denominator is generations, not acceptances. Generate 100, rewrite 50, and success is 50%, not 100%.
  3. 分子漏人審。一次 $0.30 的呼叫，若換來 $90／小時員工 20 分鐘清理，清理成本 $30。
  4. Human review is missing from the numerator. A $0.30 call that creates 20 minutes of cleanup at $90/hour costs $30 in cleanup.
  5. 重試與 refinement loop 沒進成本。第一次便宜、第五次才過關，單件成本要按第五次算。
  6. Retries and refinement loops are omitted. Cheap on the first pass, accepted on the fifth: unit cost is the fifth pass.

Agent 能不能上，McKinsey 收成一個事前篩選式：

Whether an agent should even be pointed at a task, McKinsey compresses to a screen used before spend:

P(success) > T_verify / T_do

人做 2 小時、核對 6 分鐘，門檻約 5%。核對若變成重做，T_verify → T_do，門檻變成接近 100%，多數 agent 會瞬間不經濟。量測系統必須分開記：T_verify（抽檢、點頭、小改）、T_rework（實質重寫）、T_escalate（踢回專家）。只有第一種能撐起「低成功率也划算」的不等式。後兩種要進失敗成本。

Two hours of human work and six minutes of verification sets a bar of about 5%. If verification becomes rework, T_verify → T_do and the bar approaches 100%; most agents become uneconomic on the spot. The measurement system must log three clocks separately: T_verify (spot-check, nod, small edit), T_rework (substantive rewrite), T_escalate (hand-up to an expert). Only the first clock can support “low success still pays.” The other two belong in the cost of failure.

8.5 工時這筆帳：生產力洩漏

8.5 The hours ledger: productivity leakage

「省下 30 分鐘」要同時通過四道閘，才能進 V：

“Saved 30 minutes” enters V only after four gates:

  1. 來源是系統時鐘，不是問卷。問卷會系統性高估。
  2. The clock is the system clock, not a survey. Surveys overstate systematically.
  3. 時鐘打在「接受輸出」，不是「模型吐字」。簡報 3 分鐘生成、3 小時修理，淨工時是加的。
  4. The clock stops at accepted output, not at first generation. A deck written in three minutes and repaired for three hours added hours, it did not save them.
  5. 省下的時間有去處：多處理件數、少加班、少外包，或轉去做事先指定的高價值工作。停在「比較從容」是福利，不是 ROI。
  6. The hours go somewhere: more units, less overtime, fewer contractors, or a named higher-value task specified in advance. “People feel less rushed” is a benefit. It is not ROI.
  7. 採用率夠高，總帳才加總。10% 的人真的省到、90% 的人座位費空轉，團隊層 ROI 仍可以是負的。
  8. Adoption is high enough that the team total still adds. If 10% of people truly save time and 90% of seats sit idle, team-level ROI can still be negative.
V_hours = Δt_accepted × w_loaded × n_active × ρ_redeployed

ρ_redeployed 是「省下時間實際轉成產能或少付出去的錢」的比例，通常 0.3–0.7，不要默認 1.0。這一個係數，就能讓很多 Copilot 商業論證從「必賺」變成「看採用與再配置」。

ρ_redeployed is the share of saved time that actually becomes capacity or cash not paid out. It usually sits between 0.3 and 0.7; do not default it to 1.0. That one coefficient turns many copilot business cases from “must profit” into “depends on adoption and redeployment.”

工程場景可用更硬的列，再加上關掉 7 天是否回退：

Engineering can use a harder line, plus the seven-day off test:

Δ_PR = (Δ cycle time × engineer loaded rate) − token cost − review cost

8.7 Agent 的四個核心營運 KPI

8.7 Four operating KPIs for agents

能週更、能拿來做擴／停／改範圍的，其實只要四個：

Four numbers are enough to refresh weekly and to decide scale / stop / rescope:

  1. 端到端任務成功率 — 整段工作被接受、人不用重做的比例
  2. End-to-end task success — the share of whole jobs accepted without a person redoing them
  3. 人工介入率 — 中途插手的頻率；連續下降是 agent 成熟的最清楚信號
  4. Human intervention rate — how often a person steps in mid-task; a falling series is the cleanest maturity signal
  5. 完成一件的全負載成本 — 對上舊流程全負載成本，不是對上零
  6. Fully loaded cost per completed job — versus the old fully loaded cost, not versus zero
  7. 觸發到核可完結的耗時 — 含等待與升級，不含「模型思考了幾秒」
  8. Elapsed time from trigger to accepted close — including waits and escalations, excluding “the model thought for three seconds”

L1 的 token、延遲、單步準確率是診斷指標，不是成功指標。再加兩個組合指標，專門抓「看起來忙、其實不賺錢」：

Token use, latency and step-level accuracy at L1 are diagnostic. They are not success metrics. Add two composite measures that catch “busy but not earning”:

action gap = systems still crossed after the insight + people still required to act decision throughput = decisions accelerated or automated by AI / all decisions in that flow

行動落差不降，成功率再高也只是多產報告。決策吞吐不上，說明 AI 還掛在流程外面——這正是瑞銀「集成複雜度上升」在量測上的對應物。

If the action gap does not fall, a high success rate is just more reports. If decision throughput does not rise, AI is still hanging off the side of the process — the measurement twin of UBS’s rising integration-complexity score.

8.8 量測基礎設施：沒有痕跡，就沒有歸因

8.8 Measurement infrastructure: no trace, no attribution

ROI 算不出來，經常是因為系統只記了「誰花了多少 token」，沒記「這次花錢是為了完成哪一件工作、結果如何」。最低可行儀器：

ROI is often uncomputable because the system logged “who spent how many tokens” and never logged “what job that spend was for, and how it ended.” Minimum viable instrumentation:

  • 一次 run = 為同一個目標串起來的所有 session
  • One run = every session chained to the same objective
  • 一個 work item = 一個可判定成敗的目標（關一張單、審一份合約、合併一個 PR）
  • One work item = one objective that can be judged pass/fail (close a ticket, review a contract, merge a PR)
  • 每個 work item 綁：目標、結果（接受／覆寫／回流／升級）、模型花費、主人、系統
  • Each work item carries: objective, outcome (accept / override / bounce / escalate), model spend, owner, system
  • 覆寫原因做成枚舉，進產品待辦，不當註腳
  • Override reasons are an enum that feeds the product backlog, not a footnote

有團隊把 agent trace 做成語意層：先把 session 合成 run，再切成 work item，才能從「消耗了什麼」轉成「做成了什麼」。沒有這層，財務只能看到帳單。資料欄位不必一次完美。先求 70% 的 run 能掛上 work item，再提高。等完美分攤才開始量，等於永遠不量。

Some teams build a semantic layer on agent traces: sessions roll up into runs, runs split into work items, and spend can move from “what was consumed” to “what was finished.” Without that layer, finance only sees a bill. Fields do not have to be perfect on day one. Get 70% of runs onto a work item, then raise the share. Waiting for perfect allocation before measuring is a decision never to measure.

8.9 組合管理：ROI 是門檻，不是海報

8.9 Portfolio rules: ROI is a gate, not a poster

單條工作流算得清楚之後，企業層要用投資組合規則，否則會回到紅線那種「每年重寫更樂觀的預測」。

Once a single workflow can be priced, the enterprise needs portfolio rules. Otherwise the maroon line comes back: a more optimistic forecast rewritten every year.

決策Decision 條件Condition
繼續試點Keep piloting L1 達標，L2 在爬，L3 有基線 L1 clears the bar, L2 is climbing, L3 has a baseline
准許擴一個相鄰流程Scale to one adjacent flow L4 連續 8–12 週為正，採用穩定 L4 positive for 8–12 straight weeks; adoption stable
准許跨單位複製Copy across units L4 在第二個單位複現，合規路徑已標準化 L4 reproduces in a second unit; the compliance path is now standard work

改範圍Rescope 模型沒問題，卡在資料、權限、SOP——記成集成／資料障礙，不記成「AI 沒 ROI」 The model is fine; the bind is data, permissions or SOP — log it as integration / data, not as “AI has no ROI”

季度業務檢討只帶一本證據包：效益、全負載成本、歸因方法、反事實、風險調整。Token 帳單可以附錄，不能當封面。平台投資（資料湖、閘道、評估平台）用 NPV，回收期看它讓多少條工作流的 c_unit 下降，而不是自己單獨「賺了多少」。否則平台永遠像成本中心，工作流永遠像沒有地基。

The quarterly review carries one evidence pack: benefits, fully loaded cost, attribution method, counterfactual, risk adjustment. The token bill can be an appendix. It cannot be the cover. Platform spend (lake, gateway, eval harness) uses NPV; payback is how many workflows it lowers c_unit for, not whether the platform “earned” on its own. Otherwise the platform looks like a cost centre forever and every workflow looks unfounded.

8.10 七個會讓數字好看、帳卻假的算法

8.10 Seven calculations that look good and keep the books false

  1. 用生成時間當省時，不用接受時間。
  2. Treat generation time as time saved, not time-to-accepted-output.
  3. 採用率 15%，卻按 100% 座位乘上去。
  4. Adoption is 15%, but the model multiplies as if every seat were live.
  5. 把回流件算進成功率。
  6. Count bounced-back jobs as successes.
  7. 人審、重試、事故不進 C。
  8. Leave review, retries and incidents out of C.
  9. 上線前後對比，不減同期其他變化。
  10. Use before/after with no control for concurrent change.
  11. 策略型新市場效益，用當季省人的口徑來辯護。
  12. Defend a new-market strategy case with “heads saved this quarter.”
  13. 問卷裡的「我更有效率」直接乘上全負載年薪，寫進 EBIT。
  14. Multiply a survey score of “I feel more productive” by fully loaded salary and write it into EBIT.
這七項裡占三項以上，報告就還停留在瑞銀說的「預期」，不是「實際」。
Trip three or more of these and the report is still on UBS’s maroon line — expectation — not on the blue line of what actually shipped.

8.11 收束成三句可執行的話

8.11 Three executable sentences

  1. 先選一條能量的工作流，鎖基線，定義「被接受的完成件」。選不了，就還在試點，不要談組合 ROI。
  2. Pick one measurable workflow first, lock a baseline, define an accepted completion. If you cannot, you are still in pilot. Do not talk portfolio ROI.
  3. 歸因方法寫在上線前：能實驗就實驗，不能就雙差或關掉測試；人機混成預設歸人。
  4. Write the attribution method before go-live: experiment if you can; otherwise difference-in-differences or a turn-off test. Mixed human–model output defaults to the human.
  5. 週看四個流程 KPI，月看單件淨益，季看能不能複製。只有單件經濟穩定優於基線、且能跨單位複製的流程，才配從那條 19% 的藍線往上走。
  6. Watch four flow KPIs weekly, unit net monthly, replicability quarterly. Only workflows whose unit economics beat the baseline on a sustained basis, and that can be copied across units, deserve to climb the 19% blue line.

9. ROI 的長期追蹤機制

9. Long-term ROI tracking

上線後 90 天算出第一個正數，只證明這條流曾經划算。長期問題是另一個：效益會漂移、成本會爬升、採用會回落、基線會過期。沒有固定節奏與證據包，企業會回到瑞銀紅線——每年重寫更樂觀的預測，而藍線幾乎不動。

A positive number at day 90 only proves the flow once paid. The long-term problem is different: benefits drift, costs climb, adoption slumps, baselines expire. Without a fixed cadence and an evidence pack, the firm returns to UBS’s maroon line — a more optimistic forecast rewritten every year — while the blue line barely moves.

McKinsey 的說法是把 AI「當一筆被管理的投資」來跑：固定檢討節奏、明確關卡、單一證據包同時追效益與全負載成本。能過第二年續約的專案，通常已經用同一份格式連報四個季度；只在第十個月臨時做一頁的，多半續不到。
McKinsey’s phrase is to run AI as a managed investment: a fixed review cadence, explicit gates, and one evidence pack that tracks benefits and fully loaded cost together. Programmes that clear a year-two renewal have usually filed four quarters in the same format. Programmes that send one ad-hoc deck in month ten usually do not renew.

9.1 為什麼 ROI 不能當時點數字

9.1 Why ROI cannot be a point-in-time number

長期追蹤要同時看三條曲線，而不是一個百分比：

Long-term tracking watches three curves at once, not one percentage:

  1. J 曲線。DORA 2026 在工程場景寫得很清楚：上線初期生產力先掉，再爬過舊基線。前 4–8 週的負數常常是結構性的（人在學工具、流程在改），不是專案失敗。用蜜月期或用低谷當「最終 ROI」，都會判錯。
  2. The J-curve. DORA 2026 is blunt in engineering: productivity dips after rollout, then climbs past the old baseline. A negative print in weeks 4–8 is often structural (people learning the tool, the process being rewritten), not project failure. Treating the honeymoon or the trough as “final ROI” both mis-grade the case.
  3. 衰減曲線。IDC 對 agent 的判斷是：不主動調校，績效會隨情境改變與例外堆積而下降。人審占比回升、回流率爬升、採用率從 70% 掉到 40%，單件淨益會在沒有任何人「關掉專案」的情況下由正轉負。
  4. The decay curve. IDC’s read on agents is that, without active tuning, performance degrades as context shifts and edge cases pile up. Review share rises, bounce-backs climb, adoption falls from 70% to 40%, and unit net can flip from positive to negative with nobody “turning the project off.”
  5. 成本曲線。模型每 3–6 個月換一代；token 單價可能下降，但 context 變長、refinement loop 變多、人審若沒同步下降，全負載 c_unit 仍可能上升。帳單變便宜 ≠ 做成一件變便宜。
  6. The cost curve. Frontier models move every three to six months. Token unit prices may fall, but longer context, more refinement loops and a review share that does not fall can still lift fully loaded c_unit. A cheaper bill is not a cheaper completed job.

所以長期機制的單位不是「這個專案的 ROI 是多少」，而是「這條工作流此刻在曲線的哪一段、斜率是正是負、要不要關卡放行」。

So the unit of the long-term system is not “what is this project’s ROI.” It is “where on the curve is this workflow now, is the slope up or down, and does the gate open.”

9.2 Three kinds of drift, three kinds of alarm

The third kind is the most dangerous: the dashboard is accurate and simply measuring yesterday’s definition of good. Last year success was “a summary was generated.” This year the business needs “the summary was accepted and written into the ticket.” Leave the baseline alone and ROI stays pretty while the floor is empty.

資料新鮮度是系統漂移裡常被漏掉的一塊。對 agent 而言，「檢索到正確段落」和「答案在評估當下仍為真」不是同一件事。有排程的刷新／TTL，往往比「快取放了幾天」更能約束過期錯誤。長期 ROI 儀表板因此要分開記：推理錯誤 vs 過期錯誤。後者要用刷新策略修，不是再換一個更大的模型。

Freshness is the piece of system drift most often missed. For an agent, “retrieved the right passage” and “the answer is still true at evaluation time” are different events. Scheduled refresh / TTL usually bounds staleness better than “how old is the cache.” A long-run ROI board should therefore split reasoning errors from freshness errors. The second is a refresh-policy fix, not a reason to buy a larger model.

9.3 節奏：週／月／季／年各看什麼

9.3 Cadence: what to watch weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly

節奏本身就是機制。沒有固定日期與固定格式，長期追蹤會退化成「出事前做一頁」。

The cadence is the mechanism. Without a fixed date and a fixed format, long-run tracking decays into “a slide after something breaks.”

The weekly meeting does not discuss budget. The quarterly meeting does not discuss a single incident. Mix the layers in one room and the long-run system bloats, then dies.

9.4 證據包：每季同一份，不另做簡報

9.4 The evidence pack: the same file every quarter, no extra deck

長期追蹤能活下來，是因為格式不變、財務在上線前就簽過這份格式。建議每條在產工作流固定八頁（或八個區塊），多一頁就不准進 QBR：

Long-run tracking survives because the format does not change, and finance signed that format before go-live. Eight blocks per production workflow is enough. A ninth page does not enter the QBR:

  1. 工作流定義與「被接受的完成件」
  2. Workflow definition and the accepted completion
  3. 現行基線（日期、樣本期、重估紀錄）
  4. Current baseline (date, sample window, re-estimation log)
  5. 歸因方法（實驗／雙差／關掉測試／保守歸屬）與本季有無破壞
  6. Attribution method (experiment / DiD / turn-off / conservative) and whether it broke this quarter
  7. L1–L4 走勢圖，至少連續兩個季度
  8. L1–L4 trend charts, at least two straight quarters
  9. 全負載成本拆解：推論／平台／人審／重跑／事故
  10. Fully loaded cost split: inference / platform / review / rerun / incident
  11. 效益：有效件數 × 單件價值，扣回流與覆寫
  12. Benefit: effective units × unit value, net of bounce-backs and overrides
  13. 漂移與回滾日誌
  14. Drift and rollback log
  15. 本季關卡建議：擴／停／改／換型，附觸發閾值
  16. This quarter’s gate call: scale / stop / rescope / swap, with the trigger thresholds

財務夥伴在分發前改草稿，不在會上第一次看到數字。Token 帳單只進附錄。

The finance partner edits the draft before it circulates. Nobody sees the numbers for the first time in the room. The token bill lives in an appendix.

9.5 基線要過期，而且要寫死何時過期

9.5 Baselines expire — write down when

長期最常見的造假不是虛報效益，而是拿著兩年前的人工單件成本當永遠的對照。舊流程自己也會變：人變熟、系統升級、件量結構改變。不重估基線，AI 的「省下」會越來越大，直到有人去現場看。

The most common long-run fraud is not inflated benefits. It is keeping a two-year-old manual unit cost as the forever control. The old process also moves: people get faster, systems upgrade, the mix shifts. Leave the baseline alone and AI’s “savings” swell until someone walks the floor.

建議規則：

A workable rule:

  • 每 12 個月強制重估一次基線，用最近 4–8 週無 AI（或對照組）的營運數據。
  • Force a baseline reset every 12 months from the latest 4–8 weeks of no-AI or control-group operations data.
  • SOP、核心系統、件量結構或編制任一發生重大變化，立刻重估，不等年審。
  • Reset immediately — do not wait for the annual review — if the SOP, core system, mix or headcount changes in a material way.
  • 重估後，歷史季度不回溯粉飾；只從本季起用新基線。舊季保留「當時基線下的 ROI」，並在圖上標註斷點。
  • After a reset, do not restyle historic quarters. Use the new baseline from this quarter on. Keep prior quarters as “ROI under the then-baseline” and mark the break on the chart.
  • 若已無乾淨對照組（全面鋪開），改用「關掉 7–14 天」或「抽一個單位暫停」做局部反事實，寫進證據包。
  • If there is no clean control left (full rollout), use a 7–14 day turn-off or park one unit as a local counterfactual, and write that into the pack.

9.6 生命週期：改善或退役都是健康結果

9.6 Lifecycle: improve or retire — both are healthy

Microsoft 對 agent 生命週期的寫法可直接搬到 ROI：每個在產系統都要有主人、有監測計畫、有「改善或退役」的路徑。退役不是失敗，是把錢和注意力從不再賺錢的流上拿開。

Microsoft’s agent-lifecycle language maps onto ROI directly: every production system needs an owner, a monitoring plan, and a path to improve or retire. Retirement is not failure. It is taking money and attention off a flow that no longer earns its place.

預先公布觸發閾值，避免季會變成談判：

Publish trigger thresholds in advance so the quarterly meeting does not become a negotiation:

採用回落Adoption slump 月活環比下降 ≥ 30%，或低於 60% 座位門檻連續兩個月 MoM active-use down ≥ 30%, or below the 60% seat gate for two months

觸發後只有三條路：限期改善（寫死下一個季會要看到的 L4 數字）、縮小範圍、退役。沒有「再觀察一個季度但不改任何東西」。

Once triggered there are only three roads: a time-boxed fix (the L4 number the next QBR must see, written down), a narrower scope, or retirement. There is no “watch another quarter and change nothing.”

9.7 線上評估：讓品質進入與成本同一條時間軸

9.7 Online evaluation: put quality on the same clock as cost

長期 ROI 斷掉，經常是因為成本是即時的（帳單每月來），品質是事後的（有人抱怨才知道）。機制上要讓品質也變成週頻信號：

Long-run ROI snaps when cost is live (the bill arrives monthly) and quality is retrospective (someone complains). The mechanism is to make quality a weekly signal too:

  • 生產 trace 抽樣評分（正確、完成目標、是否選對工具），不增加使用者延遲。
  • Score a sample of production traces (correctness, goal completion, right tool) without adding user-facing latency.
  • 金標集版本化：每次改提示、知識源、權限、模型，先過回歸再放量。
  • Version the gold set. Any change to prompts, knowledge sources, permissions or the model runs regression before traffic rises.
  • 金絲雀＋自動回滾：品質或單件成本突破 SLO，先縮流量，再進週會。
  • Canary plus automatic rollback: if quality or unit cost breaches the SLO, cut traffic first, then take it to the weekly meeting.
  • 儀表板同時顯示：SLO 達成率、單件成本、漂移警報、回滾事件。缺任何一項，週會只會看到「好像比較快」。
  • The board shows SLO attainment, unit cost, drift alerts and rollback events together. Drop any one and the weekly meeting only sees “it feels faster.”

這層是 L1 的長期維生系統。它不取代 L4，但沒有它，L4 的惡化會來得又急又晚——發現時已經虧了兩個季度。

This layer is L1’s long-run life support. It does not replace L4. Without it, L4 deteriorates both suddenly and late — two quarters of losses before anyone looks.

9.8 採用與能力也要縱向追，否則工時帳會偷樂

9.8 Track adoption and skill on a longitudinal clock, or the hours ledger will cheat

ρ 再配置係數不是常數。工具換代、人員輪替、培訓只做一次，流利度會在約六個月內明顯衰退，採用率從高峰回落。長期要把三條序列對在同一張圖上：

The ρ redeployment coefficient is not a constant. Tools turn over, people rotate, one-off training decays — fluency can drop sharply inside about six months, and adoption falls off its peak. Over the long run, plot three series on one chart:

  1. 採用率（週活躍／有權限人數）
  2. Adoption (weekly actives / entitled seats)
  3. 能力代理指標（評測、抽檢通過、自願使用 vs 強制）
  4. A skill proxy (evals, sampled pass rate, voluntary vs mandated use)
  5. 單件淨益
  6. Unit net

採用掉、能力掉、淨益卻仍很好看——幾乎可以確定是基線沒重估，或 ρ 還鎖在 1.0。這張圖是給變革與培訓團隊的，不是給模型團隊的。

Adoption down, skill down, unit net still pretty — the baseline has almost certainly not been reset, or ρ is still locked at 1.0. That chart belongs to change and training, not to the model team.

9.9 組合層的長期帳：從「專案 ROI」改成「流的貢獻」

9.9 The portfolio ledger: from “project ROI” to “contribution by flow”

一年以上，不要再加總五十個專案的百分比。改看：

Past the first year, stop adding fifty project percentages together. Watch:

  • 在產流數量與健康度：多少條 L4 連續為正、多少條觸發退役。
  • Count and health of production flows: how many have a run of positive L4, how many have tripped retirement.
  • 可歸因 EBIT 的集中度：是不是 80% 的帳面效益來自兩條流。過度集中就要問複製，而不是再開二十個試點。
  • Concentration of attributable EBIT: do two flows produce 80% of booked benefit? If so, the question is replication, not twenty new pilots.
  • 平台對 c_unit 的貢獻：閘道、評估、資料路徑讓多少條流的單件成本下降。平台自己不報「ROI 300%」，它報「使 N 條流的 c_unit 中位數下降 x%」。
  • The platform’s contribution to c_unit: how many flows saw unit cost fall because of the gateway, the eval harness, the data path. The platform does not report “ROI 300%.” It reports “median c_unit down x% across N flows.”
  • 決策吞吐：有多少核心決策已經被加速或自動完成。這是「AI 是否還掛在流程外面」的長期指標，對上瑞銀的集成複雜度。
  • Decision throughput: how many core decisions are now accelerated or automated. That is the long-run test of whether AI still hangs off the side of the process — the twin of UBS’s integration-complexity score.

年度資本配置用這四項，而不是用「我們今年做了多少個 AI 專案」。

Annual capital allocation uses these four, not “how many AI projects we ran this year.”

9.10 一套最小可運轉的長期機制

9.10 A minimum mechanism that actually runs

不必先建一個 AI 卓越中心才能開始追。五件事齊了就可以進入「可續約」狀態：

You do not need an AI centre of excellence before you start tracking. Five pieces are enough to enter a “renewable” state:

  1. 每條在產流有具名主人與財務夥伴，上線前簽過證據包格式。
  2. Every production flow has a named owner and a finance partner, and both signed the evidence-pack format before go-live.
  3. 週看四個 KPI＋SLO／回滾；月看單件淨益；季看同一份八區塊證據包。
  4. Four KPIs plus SLO / rollback weekly; unit net monthly; the same eight-block pack quarterly.
  5. 基線 12 個月必重估，重大變化即重估；歷史季不回溯粉飾。
  6. Baselines reset every 12 months, and immediately on a material change; historic quarters are not restyled.
  7. 生產抽樣評估＋金標回歸＋可回滾；品質與成本在同一時間軸。
  8. Sampled production evals, gold-set regression and a rollback path; quality and cost on the same clock.
  9. 觸發閾值事先鎖定；觸發後只有改善、縮範圍、退役三條路。
  10. Trigger thresholds locked in advance; once tripped, the only roads are fix, narrow, or retire.
這五件事對上瑞銀那張圖的讀法是：紅線是「沒有節奏的預期」；藍線是「有節奏、有關卡、有效益包的實際」。長期追蹤機制的工作，就是不讓已經算出來的單件淨益在無人看守的情況下靜靜翻轉。
Read against the UBS chart: the maroon line is expectation without a cadence; the blue line is actuals with a cadence, gates and an evidence pack. The job of long-term tracking is to stop a once-positive unit net from flipping in silence because nobody was watching.
